# AGENTS.md

## Project Scope

Cosmoformer is an early-stage open-source infrastructure automation project.

The project aims to provide an open-source alternative to commercial infrastructure automation platforms, with a focus on Infrastructure-as-Code lifecycle management and related automation workflows.

The architecture, technology stack, integrations, and deployment model have not yet been finalized.

No functional product release is currently available.

## Restrictions

- Do not assume that proposed features or architectural designs have been approved or implemented.
- Do not introduce programming languages, frameworks, dependencies, or architectural decisions without authorization.
- Do not create speculative implementations, placeholder infrastructure, or unnecessary scaffolding.
- Do not provision, modify, or destroy cloud infrastructure without explicit authorization.
- Do not create or modify deployments, DNS records, credentials, or external services without authorization.
- Do not expose, log, or commit secrets, credentials, private keys, or other sensitive information.
- Do not modify repository permissions, security settings, or branch protections without authorization.
- Do not publish releases, packages, container images, or deployments without authorization.
- Do not fabricate test results, implemented features, or deployment status.
- Do not perform changes outside the authorized task scope.
